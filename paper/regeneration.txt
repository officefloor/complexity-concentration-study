How to regenerate every number in the paper
  "Complexity concentration as a god-class and god-method detector"

Every number the paper prints is produced by a script under paper/scripts/ (in the harness
repository, https://github.com/officefloor/complexity-concentration-study; the scripts are not
part of this ancillary bundle) from the harness's analysis files (anc/main-run/analysis.json,
anc/mlcq-run/analysis.json, the exploratory re-analysis and the classpath reports) and from the
paper's own reductions of the per-unit sidecars (anc/paper/human.json, agreement.json, transfer.json, false_positives.json). No number the study computes is typed by hand (literature constants and the superseded harness agreement's figures are the exceptions): main.tex
quotes macros from generated/numbers.tex and includes tables from generated/tab_*.tex, both
written by scripts/tables.py; the figures are written by scripts/figures.py. If a script's
output ever disagrees with the prose, the script is the authority.

ENVIRONMENT
  The harness's virtualenv (../.venv, from ../step1_setup.sh) runs every analysis script, since
  they import the harness's tested statistics (complexity_concentration_study.stats) and rules
  (.metrics). The figure script needs matplotlib (paper/setup.sh makes paper/.venv).
  Bootstrap intervals: 2000 resamples of repositories, seed 12345, the plan's constants
  (analyse.PLAN), so re-running reproduces the same numbers exactly.

ALL AT ONCE (from paper/)
  ./regenerate.sh              # extract (minutes), check, read-outs, tables, figures
  ./build.sh                   # the PDF, abstract.txt and arxiv.tar.gz

STEP BY STEP
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/extract_main.py     results/<date>/study.units.jsonl -> data/main.json
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/check_extract.py    data/main.json against analysis.json (must print ALL OK)
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/extract_mlcq.py     results/mlcq/study.units.jsonl    -> data/mlcq.json
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/human_readout.py    data/mlcq.json -> data/human.json (prints the read-out)
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/agreement.py        data/mlcq.json -> data/agreement.json
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/transfer.py         data/main.json + data/mlcq.json -> data/transfer.json
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/false_positives.py  data/main.json + readings/ -> data/false_positives.json
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/fp_reading.py       the reading: prepare, run-model (claude -p), collect-model
                                                  (not part of regenerate.sh; its docstring says how)
  ../.venv/bin/python scripts/tables.py           -> generated/tab_*.tex, generated/numbers.tex
  .venv/bin/python   scripts/figures.py           -> figures/*.pdf

TABLES (generated/), in the order the paper places them
  tab_sample                strata, from analysis.json diagnostics (Section 4)
  tab_calibration           estimators vs PMD, both analysis.json files (Section 5.1)
  tab_explore_primary       the explore choice, analysis.json primary (Section 5.2)
  tab_confirm_primary       the primary read-out on confirm (Section 5.2)
  tab_decisions             every decision row: analysis.json (main, MLCQ) and human.json
  tab_secondary             the three secondaries (Section 5.3)
  tab_friedman              Friedman tests, both runs (Section 5.4)
  tab_classpath             the classpath reports under main-run/classpath/ (Section 5.5)
  tab_transfer              the cutoff carried across populations and the relative cut tuned on
                            each population (tuned against tuned), transfer.json (Section 5.7)
  tab_confirm_human_class_all, tab_confirm_human_method_all      human.json (Section 5.8)
  tab_agreement             agreement.json (Section 5.8; tab_agreement_groups is also written)
  tab_fp_breakdown          false_positives.json (Section 5.9); tab_fp_readings once readings exist
  Appendix A: tab_registry (main-run/oracles.json), tab_rules (oracles.json),
              tab_human_candidates_class, tab_human_candidates_method (human.json),
              tab_exploratory (analysis.json exploratory section)
  (also written, not placed: tab_explore_{class,method}_{all,pos}, tab_confirm_human_*_pos,
   tab_confirm_{npath,decor,brain,mlcq_pmd}, tab_explore_mlcq_pmd)

FIGURES (figures/, scripts/figures.py)
  Figure 1 flag_rates.pdf           data/main.json rows (the harness's per-repository cells)
  Figure 2 auc_by_signal.pdf        data/main.json class-level per-repository AUCs
  Figure 3 pooled_distribution.pdf  data/main.json per-unit z and raw values of cognitive_max
  Figure 4 decisions.pdf            analysis.json (main, MLCQ) decisions and human.json
  Figure 5 paired_mcc.pdf           data/main.json per-repository confusion counts
  Figure 6 human_agreement.pdf      data/agreement.json

INLINE NUMBERS
  Every inline number is a macro in generated/numbers.tex (about 1,100 of them), named by its source:
  prim* (the primary), npath*/decor*/brain* (secondaries), mlcqPmd* (the plan on MLCQ's
  population), humBlob*/humLongMethod* with All/Pos (the human read-out, both populations),
  agrBlob*/agrLm* (the agreement, with RelMinus* the paired differences and *MaxMcc the best any
  detector reaches), transfer* (the cutoff carried across populations: *RelMinusOther*,
  *OtherMinusOwn*, and *TunedMinusOwn* the tuned relative cut against the tuned cutoff with the
  plan's verdict on each arm), humCand* (the numbers quoted from the candidate tables), fp* (the false positives: fpTp*/fpFp*/fpFn*/fpNeither*/fpAbsFp*
  the component breakdown, fpRead*/fpKappa* the reading, fpModelReadDate the model reader's run date, *CognitiveOneFive*
  the share at PMD's method-level cognitive threshold), sizeRho* (the size correlations),
  calib*, scale*, trivial*, flag*, cp* (classpath, with cpMaxFlipsRepo*/cpRateExclMax the one repository that carries most flips
  and the rate without it), sample*, javaFiles*, mlcq* (coverage), fried*. `grep newcommand generated/numbers.tex` lists them with their values.
  Dates and commit identifiers in the timeline paragraph are provenance from the harness
  repository's git history and the analysis files' `analysed_utc` fields.
